# BTI rating system

## Goal

Rating measures repeatable investment-decision skill, not wealth, capital or one fortunate campaign.
A ₹10,000 and ₹1,00,000 player making equivalent percentage decisions receive equivalent evidence.

## Model

BTI uses a Glicko-inspired rating with rating deviation. New players begin provisional. Each completed
campaign supplies an opponent-equivalent performance derived from normalised decision accuracy,
benchmark-relative risk-adjusted outcome, horizon and scenario difficulty. The update and all inputs are
versioned and replayable.

Permanent rating settles only on completion or explicit resignation. Active campaigns show a provisional
performance estimate. Inactivity has no penalty; resignation records a loss and prevents rating shielding.

Leaderboards are segmented initially by horizon, capital band and scenario family. Ranking uses rating
with a conservative uncertainty adjustment and requires a minimum completion count.

## Skill profile

Alongside one rating, BTI retains explainable dimensions: valuation discipline, growth assessment,
downside awareness, diversification, concentration control, rebalancing and process consistency. These
drive adaptive lessons and puzzles; they are not hidden changes to the official rating.

Levels—Novice, Learner, Club Player, Advanced and Expert—describe game achievement only and never imply
professional financial qualification.
