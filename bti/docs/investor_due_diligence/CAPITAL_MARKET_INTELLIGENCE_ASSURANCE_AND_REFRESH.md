## 1. Executive answer to the investor

The correct answer is not, **“we can guarantee that BTI will always behave exactly like the real
market.”** No simulator, forecasting model or trading system can make that guarantee. If a program
could reliably predict that BEL would trade near ₹400 months in advance, it would be a market-
prediction engine, not a training simulator.

The defensible answer is:

> **BTI will treat market realism as a continuously measured engineering property. Every new
> campaign will begin from a fresh, licensed and validated market snapshot. The subsequent market
> will be generated by a versioned multi-factor simulator calibrated against point-in-time Indian-
> market history. No scenario will be published unless it passes explicit freshness, accounting,
> distributional, risk, cross-asset and gameplay-fairness gates. Every rated campaign will freeze
> its snapshot and model version so that the market cannot move the goalposts after seeing the
> player's decisions.**

This creates two different promises which must not be confused:

1. **Market anchoring:** when a new game begins, the opening BEL price should closely match the
   permitted licensed reference price as of the displayed data timestamp. The proposed production
   target is a deviation no greater than **0.25%** from the authoritative adjusted close, with a
   hard freshness gate.
2. **Market simulation:** after the game begins, BEL's simulated future price is one plausible
   outcome drawn from a calibrated distribution. It is not expected—and cannot be promised—to stay
   within 1% of the subsequently realised real-world price.

If a campaign's simulated month advances instantly, that month is **game time**, not the future
calendar month. Only a calendar-synchronised “Live Season” could re-anchor to subsequently observed
prices, and that would be a different product mode with different rules.

### Current-state disclosure

BTI v0.16.0 is a strong playable prototype, but it does **not yet satisfy the full statistical production assurance
described above**:

- its opening equity state comes from Vriddhi's validated data through **14 August 2026**;
- Vriddhi's current manifest records Yahoo Finance via `yfinance` as the research provider;
- BTI reads the promoted CSV and manifest when the service starts;
- there is no scheduled BTI real-time or daily refresh job today;
- game-month returns are generated by a coherent seeded factor model;
- the simulator is reproducible, player-independent and internally consistent;
- it has gameplay-fairness tests, but has not yet passed a full independent statistical validation
  against point-in-time Indian-market history;
- the code identifies simulation v5 while two earlier explanatory documents still name v2. This
  documentation drift must be eliminated by generating documentation provenance from the published
  release manifest.

The investors are therefore seeing **behavioural plausibility**, not yet certified market fidelity.
The next engineering phase is to convert that plausibility into auditable evidence.

## 2. What “realistic” must mean

Realism is not one property. BTI should measure four layers independently.

| Layer | Question | Current position | Production evidence required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Visual realism | Do prices, candles, ratios and news look like a market terminal? | Strong | UX and chart-contract tests |
| Causal/accounting realism | Are OHLC, valuations, portfolios, cash, risk and news mutually coherent? | Strong foundation | Invariant and reconciliation suite |
| Statistical realism | Do returns, tails, volatility, correlations, regimes and drawdowns resemble Indian markets? | Partially modelled; not certified | Historical calibration and holdout validation |
| Decision realism | Do robust investment processes outperform naive behaviour probabilistically, without guaranteed wins? | Initial fairness evidence | Multi-horizon tournament and transfer tests |

The product should use the phrase **market-faithful simulation** only after all four layers have
published evidence. “True market behaviour” should not be used as an absolute claim.

## 3. What the current engine actually does

### 3.1 Governed starting knowledge

[`VriddhiArtifacts`](../../game_engine/artifacts.py) reads:

- `research/manifest.json`;
- `grand_table_expanded.csv`;
- horizon-specific Vriddhi portfolio bundles.

The current manifest states:

- release: `refresh-2026-08-14`;
- data through: `2026-08-14`;
- provider: Yahoo Finance via `yfinance`;
- validation: passed;
- universe: 50 stocks;
- immutable SHA-256 hashes for promoted artifacts.

This gives BTI a traceable cross-sectional starting state, but not an always-on market feed.

### 3.2 Synthetic market generation

[`simulation.py`](../../game_engine/simulation.py) currently:

- creates market, sector and company-specific seeded shocks;
- pre-generates a regime schedule independently of player holdings;
- uses forecast-derived bounded drift;
- models mean reversion after unusually large monthly moves;
- generates 21 daily OHLC candles for each game month;
- evolves price, PE, PB, PEG, forecast, volatility, Sharpe, drawdown, VaR, expected shortfall,
  momentum, RSI, activity and sentiment;
- links the simulated Nifty primarily to the trimmed cross-section of constituent returns;
- gives government bonds, corporate bonds and gold different rate, liquidity, inflation and
  risk-appetite sensitivities;
- freezes the pseudo-random world by campaign seed and engine version.

The player portfolio is not an input to market generation. The engine therefore cannot inspect a
player's choice and manufacture a punishment or reward.

### 3.3 What is already defensible

- **Reproducibility:** the same seed, release and engine version reproduce the same world.
- **Causal ordering:** a decision is made and scored before the next market state is revealed.
- **Player independence:** market paths are generated without reading player holdings.
- **Basic coherence:** low/open/close/high relationships, cash and whole-share accounting, monthly
  candle bridges, portfolio valuation and benchmark progression are deterministic.
- **Explicit simulation boundary:** the application states that securities are real but gameplay
  prices, events and outcomes are simulated.
- **Initial anti-shortcut evidence:** an eight-seed, 24-month tournament recorded in
  [`PLAYTEST_01`](../playtesting/PLAYTEST_01_ENDGAME_SCOREBOARD_AND_SIMULATION_FAIRNESS.md)
  found that concentrated momentum and lowest-PEG strategies were not universal winning exploits.

### 3.4 What is not yet defensible

- “The simulator is statistically indistinguishable from the Indian market.”
- “Displayed prices are real-time or continuously delayed exchange prices.”
- “A future simulated security price will be within 1% of the future actual price.”
- “Every factor premium, correlation and regime duration has passed out-of-sample validation.”
- “The current data source and usage terms are sufficient for a scaled commercial product.”
- “The synthetic Nifty exactly reproduces the point-in-time free-float index methodology.”

## 4. The required two-clock architecture

BTI should explicitly maintain two clocks.

### 4.1 Market-data clock

This records when the real-world anchoring data was observed:

```text
market_as_of = 2026-08-27T15:30:00+05:30
feed_type = EOD_D1 | DELAYED_15M | LICENSED_REALTIME
source = NSE_DATA_VENDOR
snapshot_id = sha256(normalised_snapshot)
```

### 4.2 Game clock

This records the player's simulated investment month:

```text
campaign_month = 17 / 60
scenario_release = CMIE-2026-08-28.1
scenario_seed = server-private
```

The UI must show both. “SIM MONTH 17” must never be mistaken for a real quote timestamp.

### 4.3 Campaign rule

- **New campaign:** receives the latest promoted real-market anchor and latest approved calibration.
- **Existing rated campaign:** retains its original snapshot, universe, parameters and scenario path.
- **Engine refresh:** never rewrites an in-progress rated game.
- **Material model change:** creates a new season/version, not a silent replacement.

This is analogous to a chess engine version and a fixed starting position. Fairness requires the
position not to change midway through a rated match.

### 4.4 Vriddhi-synchronised baseline — the default release contract

BTI must never contain a hard-coded knowledge date. Its baseline selector is:

```text
BTI_BASELINE = latest successfully promoted Vriddhi release
```

This is the implemented production contract. `VriddhiArtifacts` reads
`research/manifest.json`, `grand_table_expanded.csv` and the horizon portfolio bundles when the BTI
service starts. `BTIGame.create` copies the manifest's `release_id` and `data_through` into the new
campaign. Render is configured to redeploy BTI after a commit to the repository.

The intended release sequence is therefore:

```text
Vriddhi monthly refresh
  → isolated candidate build
  → Vriddhi validation and regression gates
  → promoted manifest and hashed artifacts
  → reviewed merge to master
  → automatic BTI redeployment
  → BTI startup verifies the artifact hashes
  → BTI health reports the active Vriddhi release
  → every NEW campaign uses that release
```

Until the next successful refresh, BTI continues using the last valid Vriddhi baseline. If a refresh
fails, the old release remains active; BTI must never fall forward to a partially generated candidate.

BTI v0.16.0 implements the four release-synchronisation controls:

1. the BTI health response exposes active `release_id`, `data_through`, validation state and sync mode;
2. startup verifies all BTI-consumed artifacts against the SHA-256 values in the promoted manifest;
3. automated API tests prove that the active BTI release equals the promoted Vriddhi manifest;
4. every new campaign freezes the baseline stocks and private reference weights needed to preserve
   its original world after a newer Vriddhi release is promoted.

The player-facing label should read, for example:

> **Market intelligence baseline: latest validated Vriddhi release · Data through 14 Aug 2026**

After the next refresh, a newly created campaign should show the new date automatically. Existing
rated campaigns must continue to show and use their original date.

## 5. Production data acquisition and refresh

### 5.1 Data-source principle

`yfinance` is acceptable for prototyping and internal research. It should not be the sole source for
a commercial freshness SLA. Before a production claim, BTI should contract for data with explicit
display, simulation, storage and redistribution rights.

NSE Data & Analytics officially offers real-time Level 1/2/3 and tick-by-tick feeds, one- and
five-minute snapshots, 15-minute delayed snapshots, end-of-day/historical data and corporate data.
The applicable subscription and use agreement must match BTI's web, PWA, simulation and research
uses:

- [NSE real-time and snapshot products](https://www.nseindia.com/static/market-data/real-time-data-subscription)
- [NSE end-of-day and historical products](https://www.nseindia.com/static/market-data/eod-historical-data-subscription)
- [NSE corporate data products](https://www.nseindia.com/static/market-data/corporate-data-subscription)
- [NSE data usage and sharing policy](https://www.nseindia.com/static/market-data/nse-data-policy)
- [Nifty 50 methodology](https://www.niftyindices.com/Methodology/Method_NIFTY_Equity_Indices.pdf)

### 5.2 Recommended launch feed

For an educational strategy game, **licensed end-of-day or permitted one-day-delayed data is the
recommended first production boundary**, not tick-by-tick data. Intraday speed adds cost and legal
complexity but little value to a game whose decisions occur at monthly or multi-year intervals.

The proposed source hierarchy is:

1. licensed NSE/NSE-authorised vendor equity and index close data;
2. official corporate actions, constituent changes and company filings;
3. official/contracted fixed-income curves and valuations;
4. licensed gold reference series;
5. RBI and government macro series for rates, inflation and liquidity;
6. a secondary independent source used only for reconciliation and anomaly detection.

### 5.3 Refresh cadence

| Data | Ingest cadence | Publish SLA | Hard stale threshold |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Equity/index EOD anchor | Every trading day | Before next game day | 2 trading days |
| 15-minute delayed feed, if licensed | Every minute | Provider lag + 2 minutes | 25 minutes in market hours |
| Corporate actions | Daily and event-driven | Before affected snapshot | No unprocessed effective action |
| Constituents/weights | On announcement and effective date | Before new campaign | No stale membership |
| Fundamentals/results | Event-driven | One business day after validated filing | One reporting cycle |
| Rates/inflation/liquidity | On official release | One business day | One missed release |
| Model calibration | Monthly | After validation gates | 45 calendar days |
| Full independent review | Quarterly | Signed report | One quarter |

The practical rollout has two levels:

- **Level 1 — immediately recommended:** BTI follows the latest validated monthly Vriddhi release.
  This gives one governed source of truth and avoids a second competing research pipeline.
- **Level 2 — later, if required:** a licensed daily/EOD price-anchor layer sits in front of the
  monthly Vriddhi intelligence release. Fundamentals and forecasts remain monthly/event-driven while
  opening prices and derived risk measures are made fresher.

Level 1 is sufficient if BTI clearly displays the Vriddhi `data_through` date and does not describe
the baseline as real-time. Level 2 is required only if the product promises near-current opening
quotes between Vriddhi refreshes.

### 5.4 Pipeline

```text
Licensed/official sources
        ↓
Raw immutable landing zone (source bytes + timestamps + licence ID)
        ↓
Normalisation and security-master mapping
        ↓
Corporate-action adjustment and point-in-time joins
        ↓
Cross-source reconciliation and quality rules
        ↓
Golden Market Snapshot
        ↓
Calibration parameter build
        ↓
Statistical + gameplay validation gates
        ↓
Signed Market Intelligence Release
        ↓
New BTI campaigns only
```

Every stage must be rerunnable and content-addressed. Publication should be atomic: either the full
snapshot is promoted, or the previous healthy release remains active.

### 5.5 Golden Market Snapshot contract

Each release should store:

- security identifiers, symbol history and point-in-time universe membership;
- raw and adjusted OHLCV;
- splits, bonuses, dividends, rights, mergers and delistings;
- index price and total-return series with constituent weights;
- point-in-time earnings, book value, growth, leverage, margins and forecasts;
- rates, yield curves, inflation, liquidity and gold series;
- timestamps, time zones, source IDs, licences and transformation lineage;
- missingness, staleness, outlier and reconciliation flags;
- hashes for every input and output;
- code, schema, dependency and calibration versions.

“Latest value” tables alone are insufficient: point-in-time history is required to avoid look-ahead,
survivorship and restatement bias.

## 6. Market-fidelity model

The next simulator should preserve BTI's deterministic, player-independent interface while replacing
hand-tuned relationships with estimated, governed parameters.

### 6.1 Equity return process

A practical model is a regime-conditioned hierarchical factor system:

```text
stock return
  = expected-return state
  + market factor exposure
  + sector factor exposure
  + valuation/quality/momentum factor exposure
  + company-specific residual
  + occasional jump/event component
```

Recommended components:

- a regime transition model estimated from Indian-market history;
- EWMA or GARCH-family conditional volatility;
- heavy-tailed residuals rather than Gaussian-only shocks;
- shrinkage covariance to stabilise 50-stock correlations;
- changing beta and sector correlation by regime;
- calibrated jump frequency and magnitude;
- valuation mean reversion conditioned on earnings revisions and quality;
- explicit transaction costs, liquidity and turnover in advanced gameplay.

No method should be selected merely because it is sophisticated. A simpler model wins if it
performs better on holdout fidelity and remains explainable.

### 6.2 Fundamentals and valuations

The current prototype evolves PE and PB largely from price movement. Production should instead
maintain explicit latent/observed earnings and book-value states:

```text
PE = market price / trailing earnings per share
PB = market price / book value per share
PEG = PE / governed forward growth estimate
```

Earnings and book value change on realistic reporting/event schedules. Revisions, misses, margin
shocks and balance-sheet deterioration should drive both valuations and generated news.

### 6.3 Benchmark

The benchmark should be Nifty 50 TRI where dividends are relevant, with point-in-time constituents,
free-float weights and corporate-action treatment based on the published index methodology. A
synthetic trimmed average remains useful as a game factor, but should not be labelled an exact Nifty
replication.

### 6.4 Cross-assets

- government bonds: yield, duration, curve and inflation sensitivities;
- corporate bonds: government curve plus credit-spread and liquidity dynamics;
- gold in INR: global gold, USD/INR, real-rate, inflation and risk-off factors;
- crypto, if later introduced: a separately calibrated 24x7 heavy-tail/liquidity regime—not an
  equity residual with higher volatility.

## 7. Assurance gates

### 7.1 Freshness and price-anchor gates

Proposed non-negotiable rules for new campaigns:

- authoritative data timestamp is displayed;
- equity opening adjusted close differs by no more than **0.25%** from the licensed reference;
- index anchor differs by no more than **5 basis points**;
- no unresolved split, bonus, dividend, merger or symbol change;
- no price is older than the applicable stale threshold;
- a failed feed displays **MARKET RELEASE UNAVAILABLE** and prevents new rated campaigns;
- the system never silently substitutes an old snapshot.

The investor's ±1% BEL requirement is therefore appropriate as a **maximum opening-anchor test**;
the proposed 0.25% target is stricter. It is not appropriate as a future-path test.

### 7.2 Accounting and invariant gates

- OHLC ordering always holds;
- adjusted returns reconcile across corporate actions;
- daily candles bridge to authoritative simulated monthly closes;
- index, holdings, trades, cash and portfolio NAV reconcile exactly;
- ratios reconcile to their price/fundamental inputs;
- risk metrics use only information visible at that game time;
- generated news is traceable to a deterministic state/event.

### 7.3 Statistical fidelity gates

Measure real and synthetic holdout samples across bull, bear, sideways, crisis, recovery and
rotation periods:

- return mean, volatility, skewness, kurtosis and quantiles;
- 95%/99% VaR exceedance and expected shortfall;
- autocorrelation of returns and squared returns;
- volatility clustering and regime duration;
- pairwise/sector correlation, covariance eigenvalues and correlation spikes in sell-offs;
- drawdown depth, duration and recovery time;
- breadth, dispersion and concentration;
- valuation, growth and quality distributions;
- factor returns, reversals and decay;
- cross-asset correlations and stress response.

Use confidence intervals rather than matching a single statistic. A blinded classifier that tries
to distinguish real windows from synthetic windows is a useful aggregate diagnostic; a materially
high out-of-sample AUC signals detectable simulation artifacts.

### 7.4 Decision-fidelity gates

Run Classic, Rapid and Blitz tournaments across all horizons and thousands of governed scenarios:

- lowest PEG;
- highest Sharpe;
- momentum chasing;
- news/sentiment chasing;
- minimum volatility;
- equal weight;
- concentrated conviction;
- cash-heavy behaviour;
- random valid portfolios;
- disciplined adaptive/Vriddhi-like rebalancing.

Acceptance principle:

- no static public heuristic wins across every regime;
- disciplined adaptive processes outperform random behaviour in aggregate;
- good decisions raise success probability but never guarantee a win;
- weak decisions can occasionally win through luck and are diagnosed accordingly;
- the generator cannot condition outcomes on player holdings;
- results remain stable across reasonable parameter perturbations.

### 7.5 Out-of-sample and red-team gates

- calibration and evaluation periods are separated chronologically;
- constituent and fundamental data are point-in-time;
- at least one external reviewer receives only frozen test inputs and acceptance criteria;
- deliberate failure cases cover stale data, bad splits, missing prices, extreme gaps, delistings,
  correlation breakdown and provider outage;
- a model-risk sign-off records limitations and rejected releases.

Create a public/internal **Market Fidelity Scorecard** for every release:

| Control | Status shown |
|---|---|
| Anchor data timestamp | Exact IST time/date |
| Provider and licence class | Governed source ID |
| Universe completeness | x / 50 healthy |
| Corporate-action reconciliation | Passed/failed |
| Price reconciliation | Maximum deviation |
| Model calibration date | Exact release |
| Holdout fidelity | Passed/failed with report link |
| Fairness tournament | Passed/failed with scenario count |
| Simulation version | Immutable identifier |
| Next scheduled refresh | Exact date/time |

Operational alerts should cover feed latency, stale symbols, cross-source discrepancies, missing
filings, parameter drift, abnormal synthetic tails, repeated scenario artifacts and strategy
dominance. Publication automatically stops on a red control.

## 9. Regulatory and licensing boundary

Market-data rights and education/advice boundaries are product requirements, not paperwork to add
after engineering.

SEBI's 8 May 2026 circular on educational use of price data, effective 1 July 2026, discusses a
one-day lag for educational and awareness activities and restrictions around the use of recent
security-specific data in content that indicates future price, advice or recommendation. BTI is a
simulation game, not an adviser, but it uses real security names and decision scoring. Indian
securities counsel should approve the precise data-feed, display, generated-news and gameplay
design before a commercial launch:

- [SEBI circular: norms for sharing and usage of price data for educational purposes](https://www.sebi.gov.in/web/?file=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.sebi.gov.in%2Fsebi_data%2Fattachdocs%2Fmay-2026%2F1778242522289.pdf)

Until that review:

- retain persistent **SIMULATION MODE** labelling;
- do not call generated prices live quotes;
- do not imply future-price prediction;
- do not present the hidden reference as investment advice;
- do not ingest or redistribute a feed without documented rights;
- keep an auditable distinction between source observations, derived metrics, simulated states and
  generated narrative.

### Days 1–5 — contracts and data decision

- approve the two-clock and campaign-freeze contracts;
- obtain legal interpretation and define permitted data latency;
- select licensed primary and reconciliation providers;
- define the Golden Market Snapshot schema and source registry;

- add cross-source reconciliation and freshness circuit breakers;

### Days 13–20 — calibration engine

- assemble point-in-time calibration and holdout datasets;
- estimate regime, factor, covariance, volatility, tail and cross-asset parameters;
- replace ratio drift with earnings/book-value state transitions;
- version every parameter and transformation;
- preserve the current simulator interface for gameplay compatibility.

### Days 21–25 — assurance laboratory

- implement distribution, tail, correlation, drawdown and regime tests;
- run strategy-archetype tournaments across all modes/horizons;
- conduct leakage, survivorship and corporate-action audits;
- run adversarial/provider-outage cases;
- produce machine-readable and plain-English scorecards.

### Days 26–30 — independent evidence and controlled release

- freeze a release candidate;
- give holdout evidence and criteria to external quantitative reviewers;
- remediate only against predefined gates;
- publish limitations and rejected claims;
- release to new campaigns while preserving existing rated campaigns;
- rehearse rollback to the last healthy market release.

### Definition of done

The programme is complete only when:

1. a new campaign is anchored to a fresh, permitted, validated snapshot;
2. stale or unreconciled data cannot silently enter rated play;
3. the model passes published holdout and fairness gates;
4. an external reviewer can reproduce the scorecard from frozen inputs;
5. every campaign proves which data, calibration, simulator and scorer created it;
6. legal review confirms the data and educational-use boundary;
7. the UI communicates market timestamp, game clock and simulation status unambiguously.

## 11. Recommended investor wording

> BTI does not claim to predict the exact future price of BEL—or any security. Its purpose is to
> reproduce the decision conditions that make investing difficult: changing regimes, correlated
> risk, valuation dispersion, earnings revisions, drawdowns, noisy news and uncertain outcomes.
>
> The current prototype demonstrates a coherent, reproducible and player-independent simulated
> capital market anchored to Vriddhi's validated August 2026 knowledge release. We are now proposing
> the production Market Fidelity Programme: licensed daily market anchoring, point-in-time data,
> monthly model recalibration, statistical holdout validation, strategy stress tournaments,
> freshness circuit breakers and independent quarterly review.
>
> A new campaign's opening prices will be reconciled to the permitted authoritative market snapshot.
> Its future game path will then be frozen and synthetic, so the engine cannot move the goalposts in
> response to the player. We will publish what was measured, what passed and what remains uncertain.
> The assurance is not that the simulator knows the future; it is that it creates a current,
> statistically market-faithful and auditable environment in which sound decision processes can be
> practised under uncertainty.

## 12. Final architectural decision

BTI should **not** attempt to make its instantly played future path track future actual prices to
within ±1%. That requirement is impossible without hindsight and would undermine the game.

BTI should instead guarantee, subject to licensed-feed availability:

- **freshness and accuracy of the opening market anchor**;
- **statistical fidelity of the generated market**;
- **causal independence and immutability of rated campaigns**;
- **continuous calibration and published assurance evidence**;
- **transparent uncertainty and simulation labelling**.

That is the standard external quantitative reviewers can test, management can govern and investors
can credibly underwrite.
