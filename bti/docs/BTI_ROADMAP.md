# BTI roadmap

## Now — playable web vertical slice

- 24/36/48/60-month campaign creation;
- deterministic simulated Nifty 50 market board and stock details;
- whole-share monthly execution sheet;
- hidden feasible Vriddhi reference and equivalent-good scoring;
- separate process and market-outcome analysis;
- progress, final result, starter puzzles and lessons;
- automated campaign and temporal-integrity tests.

## Next — trustworthy product beta

- durable accounts, resume/history and Glicko-inspired rating;
- personal skill map and adaptive learning;
- post-game analysis board and bounded counterfactuals;
- scenario families, calibrated difficulty and segmented leaderboard;
- production observability, privacy workflow and security review.

## Then — Android delivery

- stable versioned HTTP facade and Google authentication;
- native mobile market, portfolio and score-reveal experience;
- offline draft resilience, signed AAB and Play Internal Testing;
- device, accessibility and store-compliance qualification.

## Later — evidence expansion

- immutable monthly point-in-time Vriddhi releases and corporate actions;
- honest historical replay through the scenario port;
- tournaments and social features only after scoring credibility and campaign completion are proven.

Success is measured first by completed moves and campaigns, score trust, next-move intent and demonstrated
skill improvement—not screen count, simulated returns or model complexity.
