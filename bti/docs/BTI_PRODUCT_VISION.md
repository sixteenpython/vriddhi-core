# BTI product vision

Beat the Index turns evidence-led portfolio construction into a learnable monthly strategy game.
The player makes one irreversible allocation decision per month, sees what happened, receives a
bounded diagnosis and builds a skill rating over complete games.

BTI is not a broker, tip service, return promise or prediction of investment success. It is a
simulated educational product. Vriddhi is the private research authority; BTI is the game layer.

## North star

Help a player learn whether a portfolio decision improved the probability of superior long-term,
risk-adjusted returns—without revealing a magic answer before the decision.

## MVP success

- a new player completes the first move without instruction from a human;
- the same frozen game can always be reproduced and audited;
- feedback explains one useful strength and one useful improvement in plain English;
- completing a game feels consequential while real capital is never at risk.
